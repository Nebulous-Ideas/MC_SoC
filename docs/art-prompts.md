# Art prompts: the sock puppet rapper

For an image generator. The mascot is an invented character, a hand-sewn sock puppet who raps about what went wrong and
what we learned. It stands for the project's idea: small, homemade, honest about mistakes, with a person approving the
final cut.

## Rules for every prompt

- **No readable text or numbers in the picture.** Add the title in your editor; generators spell badly and it dates the image.
- **No real people, no sound-alikes, no real logos, brands, bands or album covers.** Do not name an artist in a prompt, not even as a style reference.
- **No addresses, hostnames or screenshots** of real systems.
- **One look across the set:** a worn cassette-era illustration, flat shapes, visible grain, one warm lamp against a blue-black night. It matches the album art in the song folder.
- Say what you used. A generated image goes in with the tool and date in its file note, the same as a work's `made_with`.

## The character

A tall tube sock in mismatched stripes, one darned heel, two big sewn-on button eyes (different sizes), a felt tongue, a tiny
folded-paper headband. It holds a toy microphone made from a spool and a cork. It has no hands to speak of: just the sock arm,
raised mid-verse. Friendly and a little tired, not edgy.

## README banner (wide, 3:1)

> Wide cassette-era illustration, flat shapes and film grain. A hand-sewn striped sock puppet in mismatched button eyes raps
> into a spool-and-cork microphone, mid-verse, one arm raised. Behind it, a cluttered desk under a single warm lamp: a
> mug, a stack of index cards, a small stamp pad with a plain round stamp, a tin can with pencils. Blue-black night window
> behind. Empty space on the left third for a title. No text, no numbers, no logos, no real people.

## Social card (1200x630)

> Same sock puppet, centred, shoulders up, lit by one warm lamp, against a deep blue-black background with a few tiny
> glowing dots like distant windows. A single paper speech bubble above it containing only a small drawn checkmark, no
> letters. Flat shapes, grain, generous margins so a platform's crop does not cut the character. No text.

## Avatar (square, reads at 64 px)

> Close crop of the sock puppet's face: two mismatched button eyes, felt tongue out, folded paper headband. Flat warm lamp
> on one side, blue-black on the other. Bold simple shapes, thick outline, no background detail, no text.

## Small variants (optional)

- **Verify:** the puppet squinting through a magnifying glass at a row of index cards.
- **Sign-off:** a hand (a person's, no face) lowering a plain round stamp onto a card while the puppet waits.
- **Redact:** the puppet holding up a black marker, a few cards with bars across them.
- **Share:** the puppet passing a small box across a table to another, different puppet.

## After you have an image


- Keep the source file small (under about 300 KB); `*.png`/`*.jpg` are fine, audio is not.
- Record the generator, date and the prompt used in a note next to it, so the picture is as traceable as the rest of the work.
